Dalton's Model of an Atom

The Solid Sphere Model

John Dalton was an English chemist who transformed Democritus's philosophical idea into a scientific theory based on measurable evidence and chemical reactions.

Postulates:

  • All matter is composed of extremely small particles called atoms.
  • Atoms of a given element are identical in size, mass, and properties.
  • Atoms cannot be subdivided, created, or destroyed.
  • Atoms of different elements combine in simple whole-number ratios.

His "Billiard Ball" model pictured the atom as a solid, hard sphere with no internal structure—a revolutionary step for 19th-century science.
