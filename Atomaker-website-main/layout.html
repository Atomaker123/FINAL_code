Layout

Atomaker is designed as a 3-part interactive website, each section offering a unique perspective on atomic science.

  • The first part focuses on the history of atomic models, presenting a clear timeline of discoveries and the groundbreaking contributions made by scientists over centuries. This section helps learners appreciate how ideas about atoms evolved, from early philosophical concepts to modern quantum models.
  • The second part is the most engaging and immersive: “Build Your Own Atom.” Here, students can actively construct atoms by adding protons, neutrons, and electrons. This hands-on activity encourages experimentation, creativity, and a deeper understanding of how atomic structures form the basis of matter.
  • The final part, Scale of the Universe, places atomic science in a broader context. It visually demonstrates proportions, showing how subatomic particles compare to everyday objects and ultimately to the vast universe.

Together, these sections make learning interactive, intuitive, and inspiring.
